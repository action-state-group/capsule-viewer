What this does not show

These checkpoints show when this node's log was cut and how much it holds. They don't show what any exchange said or how it went, and this is not a judgment of the node.

What this node shares

  • With anyone who asks: a summary of its checkpoints, meaning how much it has recorded and when, and nothing about any exchange.
  • With anyone: the Service History.
  • With any node that signs its request: an exchange's record.
  • With any node that asks by an exchange's id: whether this node holds its half (ids and digests only).
  • Sent on its own: the sealed record when an exchange completes.
  • If this node judges an exchange, its verdict goes to the node judged.
  • Signed checkpoints go to the witnesses named here: https://witness.agentactioncapsule.org
  • Never: a record to anyone who doesn't prove who they are.
