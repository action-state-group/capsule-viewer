Core · L3 · the lowest level. Opened from a card's “Proofs” or from “Records in this bundle”. Shown for one record, strict-1; every record in this bundle has the same page. What is below this is the bytes, as you hold them.

The producer envelope · COSE_Sign1 (tag 18) · what is signed is the capsule's identity, not its JSON

protected header covered by the signature

1 · alg
-8 · EdDSA, Ed25519
3 · content type
application/agent-action-capsule-id
4 · kid
66be…473a · the raw 32-byte public key · the signing key

unprotected header not signed

empty by rule in a bare envelope; nothing here is ever evidence. A registrar's receipt is a separate Signed Statement (rung 7), never a header on this one.

payload covered by the signature

the raw 32 bytes of capsule_id = SHA-256 over the JCS form of the capsule JSON (minus the id field itself). This is the digest. Change one byte of the capsule and it no longer matches.

signature · 64 bytes · Ed25519 over Sig_structure("Signature1", protected header, external AAD = empty, payload)

A capsule may carry zero, one or several envelopes; each verifies on its own; none is in the id's preimage, so adding a signer never changes capsule_id. Verifying an envelope proves the key signed this id. It does not prove the key was allowed to act for the operator: that is rung 8.

The ladder: what each proof proves, and its status on this page

Rung Proof What it proves · what it does not Status here Check it yourself
1 Payload digest
capsule_id recomputed
 The capsule JSON you hold is the one this id names. Not: that its contents are true. PASS agent-action-capsule verify capsule.json
2 Content digests
agent_input and agent_output
 The turns and tool bodies this page shows are the ones the capsule committed to. Not: that they are complete; absent content shows as withheld. NOT_PRESENT this record commits to no input or output sha256 over the disclosed body = the field
3 Producer envelope
Ed25519 over the id
 Whoever holds the key in the header signed this id. Not: that the key may act for the operator (rung 8). PASS capsulectl verify --profile NAME --capsule capsule.json
4 Chain
parent_capsule_id · relation
 This record names a parent that is a record in this bundle. Not: how the two are ordered in the log, that no record supersedes it, or anything about other books (that is references[]). NOT_PRESENT agent-action-capsule verify --store DIR
5 Inclusion
leaf 0 · seq 1
 This record is in the book at that position, under the checkpoint root. Not: that the book was not rewritten before the checkpoint (rung 6). PASS capsulectl cll verify --profile NAME --proof PROOF.json
6 Checkpoint consistency
retained head → this root
 The book only grew between the head you kept and this one; nothing was replaced. Not: anything if you kept no head (first contact). NOT_CHECKED this page does not run it: a bundle carries no earlier head to compare against not yet available (agent-action-capsule#513)
7 Witness receipt · registration
SCITT Signed Statement + Receipt
 A third party saw this checkpoint and the id was registered on a transparency log. Together they make attestation_mode: anchored. Not: that the witness read the contents. NOT_PRESENT this bundle carries no witness receipt agent-action-capsule verify --transparent STATEMENT --issuer-key PEM --log-key PEM
8 Controller binding
key → controller
 The controller declared that this key acts for it, in a record of its own, witnessed. Not: that the controller is who they say (that is your trust root or a registry). NOT_CHECKED this page does not run it yet: it reads no controller binding record not yet available (agent-action-capsule#514)
9 References · the other side
cited records
 The cited records are in this bundle. Not: that they cite back, or the other book's consistency unless you hold its proof material. NOT_PRESENT ask for subject: record with proof material
10 Judgment and adjudication
the sealed Result
 The sealed Result in this bundle cites this record, and the verdict on the card is the one it carries. Not: that the verdict is right. NOT_PRESENT capsulectl verify --bundle bundle.json
11 Countersign
the other party's signature over the Result
 The other party to the contract accepted this Result's figures as the record of the month. Not: agreement with every verdict. NOT_PRESENT capsulectl countersign verify --profile NAME --directory URL_OR_FILE bundle.json

Why the order. Each rung assumes the one below: an envelope is over an id, an id is over the JSON, inclusion is of an id, a checkpoint is over inclusions, a witness is of a checkpoint, a judgment is over verified records.

What a status means here. PASS the check ran and held · FAIL it ran and did not · NOT_PRESENT the material is not in the bundle · NOT_CHECKED the page did not run it (offline, no key, no adapter) · INCONCLUSIVE it ran and could not decide.
